Hint #1: Formula Identifier

Recall the range equation for a projectile launched over flat terrain:
R = (v² * sin(2θ)) / g
Where v = 25 m/s, θ = 45°, and gravity g ≈ 9.81 m/s².
2

Hint #2: Trigonometric Simplification

Since θ = 45°, calculate 2θ = 90°. Note that sin(90°) = 1. This simplifies your calculation significantly!

Complete Worked Solution

Step 1: Square the velocity: 25² = 625.

Step 2: Multiply by sin(90°): 625 * 1 = 625.

Step 3: Divide by gravitational acceleration: 625 / 9.81 = 63.71 meters.

Correct Choice: B) 63.7 meters
